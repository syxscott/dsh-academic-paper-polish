# Revised conodont ranges of the Yangtze Basin: a second candidate

## Abstract

A revised range chart for the Middle Ordovician of the Yangtze Basin is presented. The study is based on new material and covers several sections. The results show that the diversity increased up-section. We suggest that the genus Panderodus was widespread in the basin.

## Introduction

Conodont elements are used for biostratigraphy in the Ordovician of the Yangtze Basin. The aims of this paper are to revise the ranges of the genus Panderodus. We reviewed the published records and added new material. The taxonomy of the genus remains unclear in several intervals.

## Methods

Twelve samples were collected from three sections of the Yangtze Basin. The samples were processed in dilute hydrochloric acid and the residues were sieved. All elements were picked under a stereo microscope and measured at ×40 magnification. The measurements were processed in Python 3.11 and outliers were excluded.

## Results

The revised ranges extend the known range of Panderodus by one graptolite zone. The assemblage contains 24 species and 180 individuals in the revised interval. The elements range from 120 µm to 480 µm in length.

## Discussion

The revised ranges are consistent with the regional literature and with the new material. We therefore propose that the genus was widespread in the Yangtze Basin. The paleogeographic implications of the revision are important for regional correlation.

## Conclusion

We found that the genus Panderodus was widespread in the Yangtze Basin during the Middle Ordovician. The revised ranges are consistent with the regional literature.
